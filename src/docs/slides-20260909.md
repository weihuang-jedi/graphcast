## Slide 2: Executive Summary & Project Goal

**Primary Objective:** Stabilize an end-to-end 3D GraphCast model trained on Log-State variables ($\ln P, Q, \ln T, U, V, W$) for long-range ($240\text{h}$ / 10-day) autoregressive rollouts.

**Key Achievements:**

* **Overcame Early Training Collapse:** Eliminated Epoch 4 field pinning where $T$ and $Q$ flattened ($0.0000$ ACC) by restructuring normalization and momentum weights.
* **Mastered Mass & Pressure Drift:** Developed an in-graph soft zero-mean projection and explicit surface pressure penalty ($\text{loss\_surf\_mass}$), reducing Day 5 $P$ bias from **$+18.82\text{ hPa}$ to $-3.39\text{ hPa}$**.
* **Validated 10-Day Skill:** Achieved an operational-grade Day 5 Temperature ACC of **$0.8513$** and Moisture ACC of **$0.8108$**.

## Slide 3: Core Technical Breakthroughs

```
[1. Standardization & Weights] ---> [2. 3D Navier-Stokes Loss] ---> [3. Soft Mass Projection]
- Log-state Standardization         - Continuity: R_cont              - In-graph Zero-Mean Δ(ln P)
- Bounded momentum penalties        - Vertical Hydrostatic           - Level 0 Surface Mass Penalty

```

* **3D Mass Continuity Residual:** Formulated non-dimensionalized log-density advection:

$$\nabla(\ln \rho) = \nabla(\ln P) - \nabla(\ln T), \quad R_{\text{cont}} = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla(\ln\rho) + \nabla \cdot \mathbf{u}}{10^{-4} \text{ s}^{-1}}$$


* **Unrolled Backpropagation (BPTT):** Extended training unrolling from $k=2$ ($12\text{h}$) to $k=4$ ($24\text{h}$) steps to teach the neural network multi-step error accumulation dynamics.

## Slide 7: Operational Strength Analysis

### Strategic Advantages of Our Optimized M6 Model

1. **Unmatched Mass Conservation:** Incorporating an explicit in-graph soft projection directly eliminates the systemic global pressure drop typical of long autoregressive rollouts.
2. **Computational Efficiency on M6 Mesh:** Using $40,962$ icosahedral nodes yields a **$25\times$ memory reduction** over 0.25° lat-lon grids, enabling $24\text{h}$ BPTT unrolling on a single H100 GPU.
3. **Robust Thermodynamic Boundaries:** Moisture ($Q$) retains realistic physical limits ($0.00 \dots 27.5\text{ g/kg}$) across 40 unrolled steps without grid-point drying or artificial saturation.
